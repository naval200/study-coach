Plan changes

You can change the plan. The coach makes sure that the change does not break it.

It is easy to suggest a change. The coach applies changes only at set times.

Four types of change

TypeExampleWhen to apply
tweakUse a different resource. Drop a stretch item.Any day
swapReplace a day or topicWeekly review
replanAdd a new block or deliverable. Make the plan longer or shorter.Checkpoints
goalChange the goalCheckpoints, after a wait (default 7 days)

You can change these times in STUDY.md.

When you find something new

For example: a paper, a job post, or a course. The coach asks two questions:

  1. Does it help the goal?
  2. Does it help the deliverable for this week?

Then it puts the item in one of three places:

  • Helps this week: apply it now as a tweak.
  • Helps the goal, not this week: add it to proposals.md. Decide at the next review.
  • Does not help the goal: put it in the parking lot, with a date to look at it again.

The coach tells you where the item went in one line. You then go back to your block.

How the coach applies a change

  1. If you add work, you must remove work of the same size.
  2. The change applies from today or later. Past days do not change.
  3. The coach edits the plan and the day files.
  4. It adds a line to changes.md: date, type, change, reason, cost.
  5. It shows the new pace.
  6. For a replan or goal change, it shows the edits and asks you first.

Warnings

  • Too many swaps in one week, or 2 goal changes in 30 days: the coach tells you. It asks if the plan is wrong, or if new things pull you away.
  • Behind on the deliverable: finish it first. A scope cut is better than a swap.

When the coach suggests a change

  • Two or more days were much harder or easier than planned.
  • Your weekly hours or your goal changed.
  • A check found a missing basic concept.
  • You missed too many days in a row.
  • A checkpoint day arrived.
