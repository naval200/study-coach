Study Coach

A coach that helps you follow your own study plan.

New here? See the walkthrough: one learner, four weeks, every command she typed.

What it is

Study Coach is a skill for Claude. It reads a folder of markdown files. These files hold your study plan, your daily logs, and your notes.

The coach does not teach new material. It keeps you on your plan. It tells you where you are. It tests what you know. It helps you write posts about your progress.

What it does

TaskResult
Make a plan from your goal (Study Planner)A plan for only what you do not know yet
Tell you what to do todayA short list of tasks for the day
Record your workTicks and notes in your day file
Show your progressNumbers: day, pace, streak, reviews due
Test a conceptA grade and a date for the next review
Change the planA logged change that does not break the plan
Make postsA blog draft and short posts for each platform

Rules the coach follows

  • Keep answers short.
  • Use real numbers. Do not invent progress.
  • If you are behind, say so. Then apply the rule. Do not blame.
  • Do not accept "I get it" as proof. Ask a question.
  • Do not stop a time block for a new idea.
  • Your rules in STUDY.md are more important than the coach defaults.

How to start

No plan yet? Use the planner first: /study-planner <your goal>. It finds what you need to learn and writes the plan. See Make a plan.

  1. Write your study plan in a markdown file, or make one with the planner.
  2. Tell Claude: /study-coach init plan.md.
  3. Answer the setup questions (start date, hours, goal).
  4. Each day, tell Claude: /study-coach today.
