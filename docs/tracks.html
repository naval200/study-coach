Tracks

Real study plans run with Study Coach. Days done and days left are worked out from the start date.

Add your track

Add one entry to the tracks list at the bottom of this page: a name, your goal, your start date, your number of days, and your dashboard link. Tracks use the start and days values from your STUDY.md.
