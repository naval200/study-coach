# Intro to SQL — 3-day sample

### Day 1 · Mon 5 Jan — SELECT and WHERE
- **Learn:** SQLBolt lessons 1–4.
- **Build:** 10 queries against a sample database.
- **Done when:** you can filter and sort without looking anything up.

### Day 2 · Tue 6 Jan — JOINs
- **Learn:** SQLBolt lessons 6–7.
- **Build:** 5 join queries; one LEFT JOIN that finds missing rows.
- **Done when:** you can explain INNER vs LEFT JOIN with an example.

### Day 3 · Wed 7 Jan — GROUP BY
- **Learn:** SQLBolt lessons 10–11.
- **Build:** a small report with GROUP BY and HAVING.
- **Build (stretch):** add a window function.
- **Done when:** the report runs and you can explain HAVING vs WHERE.
